Super Scratch RPG

This was meant to be a life simulator of sorts. Something along the lines of Grand Theft Auto's realism.
The version here, 1.7, is extremely basic with only a house, a school, and an office building. 100% completion is literally just buying the two
items in the shop, and completing one quest.

Controls: Arrow Keys (Mouse for menu and quest1)
